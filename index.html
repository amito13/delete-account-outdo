Delete Your Account

Instructions for permanently deleting your OUTDO account and associated data.

From the app

Open OUTDO → Profile → Settings → Delete Account
  • Open the OUTDO app on your device and sign in.
  • Tap on Profile from the bottom or side navigation.
  • Go to Settings.
  • Select Delete Account.
  • Confirm the deletion when prompted. Your account and data will be permanently removed.

What gets deleted

  • Your profile information (name, email, photo)
  • Your activity history and saved preferences
  • Any content you created within the app
Please note Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. Some information may be retained for a limited period as required by law or for fraud prevention, after which it will be deleted.

Need help?

If you're unable to delete your account from the app or have questions about your data, contact us at support@outdoapp.com.
